Evidence gaps

The eventual analysis will identify areas where global evidence is substantial but African evidence is limited, while explicitly reporting uncertainty and avoiding claims that the data cannot support.

Sources & methodology

Every observation will carry its definition, source, publication year, dataset/cohort, population, sample size where available, and access date. Research statistics will be added only after the methodology and inclusion/exclusion criteria are finalized.
